# NEUROPRISON
## An Algorithmic Panopticon for the Stage
### By Robert B. Lisek

---

## Core Concept

An immersive performance where the audience becomes both prisoner and guard of a live, algorithmic system. A "phygital" panopticon making invisible architectures of AI control and surveillance tangible and visceral.

## The Mechanism

- Audience equipped with biometric sensors (EEG, GSR, eye-tracking) upon entry
- Real-time physiological data (stress, focus, gaze) fed into multi-agent AI system - the "Algorithmic Warden"
- AI narrates experience, issues commands, assigns collective risk scores based on compliance

## Immersive Experience Components

### Quantum Mirror
AI-generated video projections creating unsettling digital doppelgangers from live feeds

### Stochastic Soundscape
Raw biometric data modulates soundscape of server noise and distorted heartbeats

### Embodied Algorithms
Live performers guided by AI enact patterns of control, triggering AR overlays

## Sensory System

| Sensor | Device | Measures | Role |
|--------|--------|----------|------|
| EEG | Muse 2 Headband | Focus vs Relaxation | Collective focus metric for AI Warden |
| GSR | BITalino Kit | Arousal & Stress | Core stress signal for soundscape |
| Eye-tracking | Software-based | Attention & Gaze | Secondary data stream |

## Data Flow

1. **Sensors** capture biometric data
2. **Data** streamed to central AI "Conductor"
3. **AI** analyzes collective state
4. **System Responds** - modifying sound, visuals, performer instructions
5. **Audience** reacts physiologically, closing the loop

## Realization Phases

- **Phase 1**: Core Technology & Proof-of-Concept (5-min demo)
- **Phase 2**: System Integration & Artistic Development
- **Phase 3**: Calibration & Narrative Arc
- **Phase 4**: Staging & Premiere (May 2026)

## Technical Stack

- **Audio**: SuperCollider, Pure Data, Max/MSP
- **Visuals**: TouchDesigner, Processing
- **AI Backend**: Python (TensorFlow/PyTorch)
- **Data Protocol**: OSC (Open Sound Control)
- **Sensors**: Muse SDK, BITalino API

## Critical Proposition

The audience's collective biometric state directly fuels the system that surveils them. Creates a terrifying feedback loop: resistance strengthens the system, exposing complicity in digital control.
